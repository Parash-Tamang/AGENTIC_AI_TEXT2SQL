Plaintext

```
Table: CustomerAddress
Context: Cross-reference table mapping customers to their address(es).
Fields:
- CustomerID: Primary key. Foreign key to Customer.CustomerID. Links to Customer.CustomerID.
- AddressID: Primary key. Foreign key to Address.AddressID. Links to Address.AddressID.
- AddressType: The kind of Address. One of: Archive, Billing, Home, Main Office, Primary, Shipping.
- rowguid: ROWGUIDCOL number uniquely identifying the record.
- ModifiedDate: Date and time the record was last updated.
```

## 2. Product (The Entity Table)
This table is complex (many columns). The "Clean Doc" highlights the semantic fields users actually search for (Color, Size, Price), while the metadata keeps the full schema ready for SQL generation.
ID: `AdventureWorksLT2019.SalesLT.Product`
Document (Clean Searchable Text):
Plaintext

```
Table: Product
Context: Products sold or used in the manufacturing of sold products.
Fields:
- ProductID: Primary key for Product records.
- Name: Name of the product.
- ProductNumber: Unique product identification number.
- Color: Product color.
- StandardCost: Standard cost of the product.
- ListPrice: Selling price.
- Size: Product size.
- Weight: Product weight.
- ProductCategoryID: Foreign key to ProductCategory. Links to ProductCategory.ProductCategoryID.
- ProductModelID: Foreign key to ProductModel. Links to ProductModel.ProductModelID.
- SellStartDate: Date the product was available for sale.
- SellEndDate: Date the product was no longer available.
- DiscontinuedDate: Date the product was discontinued.
```
